# About TechHub

TechHub was founded in 2018 with a vision to democratize cutting-edge technology for enterprises and small businesses alike. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, we have expanded to a global team operating across three continents.

## Our Mission
To engineer frictionless, scalable, and secure software ecosystems that empower organizations to achieve peak operational efficiency and accelerate digital transformation.

## Our Vision
To become the definitive backbone of global corporate infrastructure, blending advanced artificial intelligence with intuitive user-centric design.

## Core Milestones
- **2018:** Founded by a team of 4 software engineers and data scientists.
- **2020:** Launched our flagship enterprise platform, NexusCore, securing Series A funding ($12M).
- **2022:** Expanded operations into EMEA and APAC regions; achieved 1 million active daily enterprise users.
- **2024:** Closed Series C funding ($85M), valuing TechHub at $1.2 Billion. Secured ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II compliance certifications.
- **2026:** Current footprint stands at over 500 enterprise customers globally, spanning healthcare, finance, and logistics sectors.

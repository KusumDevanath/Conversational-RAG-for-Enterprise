# TechHub Culture and Values

Our culture is defined not by perks or ping-pong tables, but by the shared principles that guide our everyday engineering, product, and business decisions.

## Our Core Principles
1. **Radical Transparency:** We default to open communication. Financial metrics, board decks, roadmap pivots, and post-mortems are accessible to all team members.
2. **Bias for Action:** Shipping a robust, 80%-complete feature today is infinitely better than delivering a 100%-perfect feature six months from now. 
3. **Customer Obsession:** We spend our intellectual energy understanding our users' deepest operational pain points, rather than reacting to competitors.
4. **Psychological Safety:** We promote an environment where anyone can voice dissent or call out architectural flaws, completely free of ego.
